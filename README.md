# Excel-Project
TITLE :Restaurant Sales Data Analysis 

DATASET DESCRIPTION :
This dataset contains transactional sales records for a multi-location restaurant chain across major European cities. It tracks order-level details, revenue, fulfillment channels, payment methods, and store manager performance to analyze overall business growth and regional customer preferences
        
BUSINESS QUESTIONS :

1. Which product brings the most revenue?
2. What is the Total sales performance by Manager? Which manager brings out Highest sale?
3. How do customer Payment preferences differ between In-Store and Online Sales?
4. Which city generates highest Total sales volume?
5.How are purchase types distributed across different cities?

KEY FINDINGS :

1.	Burgers dominate total revenue by product, followed by Beverages, while side items
 (Fries, Desserts) generate significally lower revenue.
2.	Jesse Pinkman leads total revenue performance among all store managers, closely
 followed by Walter White.
3.	Credit Cards are heavily preferred for Online purchases, whereas Cash and
 Gift Cards drive the majority of In-Store transactions.
4.	Lisbon generates the highest total sales volume (quantity sold) and order count, 
followed by London and Berlin.
5.	Channel Distribution by City (Q5): Online orders dominate in Lisbon and Berlin, 
while In-Store sales form the largest share in London and Paris.

RECOMMENDATIONS
1. Bundle Popular Items: Pair burgers with sides or drinks to raise average spend per order.
2. Manager Best Practices: Have top-performing managers (Jesse Pinkman), lower-performing 
   store branches.
3.  Target Regional Channels: Expand online delivery in Lisbon/Berlin and focus on in-store promos in London/Paris.
4. Optimize Payments: Fast cash processing for stores and improve  digital payments online.
